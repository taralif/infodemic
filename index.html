INFODEMIC
the only feed that shows its work

Help us test Infodemic

Infodemic is a game about working out what's real in a social feed. You and a few other people play together in the same room, each on your own device: you rate a feed of real claims on instinct, then investigate whatever you disagreed about. There's no answer key — a claim only settles when your table can justify the call.

2–4 players ~20 min gameplay Phone, laptop or tablet Ages 11+
