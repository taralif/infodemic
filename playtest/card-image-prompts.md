# Stylized Card Prompts — for ChatGPT image generation
*Feed Drop #1 · 16 cards. Workflow: paste the MASTER STYLE prompt once, generate card 1, then say "same template, next card:" and paste each card block. Keeping one chat = consistent style. Print at 4.7" × 2.4" (landscape) to fit the board slots.*

**Text warning:** image models garble small text. After each card, check the claim line is *exactly* legible. If garbled, reply "regenerate, fix the text: [exact text]". If it won't behave, generate the card art with NO text and add text yourself in Canva over the image.

---

## MASTER STYLE (paste first)

> Design a landscape trading-card for a serious board game about misinformation, 3:2 ratio, **for print: clean white background**. Style: newspaper-editorial meets detective dossier — near-black serif type (#141414), mid-grey secondary text (#6b6b6b), thin light-grey rules. Mood: prestige broadsheet, restrained, zero clip-art brightness.
>
> Layout, consistent on every card: top-left a small uppercase SUBJECT tag in letterspaced sans-serif; top-right the card number (like "F·1") in grey. Center-left: the claim text in italic serif, quoted, max 3 lines, clearly legible black on white. Bottom: a thin divider line, then a one-line source note in small grey sans-serif. Right third of the card: an illustrated vignette related to the claim — desaturated, moody, slightly grainy, like a documentary still or newsprint photo, sitting on the white card like a placed photograph with a hairline border. No logos or real platform UI. No real people's faces.
>
> The card must stay color-neutral — no red/green/yellow anywhere (players mark those with stickers during play). Confirm you're ready for card 1.
