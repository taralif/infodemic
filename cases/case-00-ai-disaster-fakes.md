# Case File #0 — "The Girl and the Puppy" (AI-generated disaster fakes)
*DRAFT FOR TARA'S VETTING — every claim needs her sign-off before play. Low sensitivity tier. Topic: unanimous #1 community concern (AI fakes).*
*Real events: Hurricane Helene aftermath, U.S. Southeast, Oct 2024. All claims paraphrased from cited coverage; no verbatim text.*

## The story (facilitator/answer-key context — not shown to players)
After Hurricane Helene devastated parts of the U.S. Southeast in late September 2024, an AI-generated image of a crying girl in a life vest holding a puppy in a rescue boat went viral, often attached to claims that the federal government had abandoned victims. Fact-checkers (Snopes, PolitiFact, Full Fact) confirmed it was AI-generated — detection tools scored it 99.5% likely synthetic, and versions showed telltale inconsistencies. Some sharers kept posting it even after learning it was fake, arguing it "represented" real suffering. Meanwhile, the real devastation was extensively documented by news photographers, and real-but-decontextualized figures (like FEMA's $750 immediate assistance payment) circulated as evidence of neglect.
